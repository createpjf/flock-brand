To add other weights (35 Thin, 45 Light, 95 ExtraBold, 105 Heavy):
  curl -sSL -o AlibabaPuHuiTi-3-XX-WEIGHT.woff2 \
    "https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/alibabapuhuiti-3-XX-weight@1.0.0/AlibabaPuHuiTi-3-XX-WEIGHT.woff2"

  Replace XX-weight with: 35-thin, 45-light, 95-extrabold, 105-heavy
  Filename casing follows the package's actual filename (capitalized weight name).

Also available formats from the same CDN packages: .otf .ttf .woff .eot
WOFF2 is preferred for web — best compression, supported by all modern browsers.
